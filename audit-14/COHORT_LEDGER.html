How to read a hit

A card is a hit when the product sold more over the window than its own trailing rate implied it would — a lift of 1.00× or better against the baseline the card itself published. It is not "the store ordered it" and not "the product sold something". The baseline is the SKU's own recent run-rate over open days, so a product that was always going to sell is not credited to the engine.

A card scored at four horizons is one decision watched for four months, not four decisions. The engine is not re-run per horizon; re-running it would be a different store on a different day.
