Engine Three · the signal ensemble · and the route to $1,000 a store

Ten families are ranked on four literals

The eleven families in the UI are not broken plumbing — the plumbing works. They are broken economics: ten of them quote a hand-written confidence, so they cannot be compared with the one family that earns its number, and the slate is carved up by family instead of by value.

Date 5 October 2026 Read in source app/recommender/families.py · pipeline.py · app/signals/ Baseline ~$370 per store per month, from one family of eleven

The four numbers that explain everything

app/recommender/families.py

  cold_box    confidence=0.55     ← weather. a literal.
  pre_stock   confidence=0.55     ← calendar, sports, news. a literal.
  bundles     confidence=0.7      ← bundle. a literal.
  stock_new   confidence=0.6      ← new & trending. a literal.

  ExtraPick.units_at_stake = None for bundles

A hand-written confidence cannot be ranked against a measured one. So pipeline.py does the only thing it can — it gives these families reserved slots: limit = max_recommendations − len(extra). A weak weather card therefore displaces a strong restock card, and a genuinely large weather opportunity can never take more than one slot. That is why the slate looks like restock: the other families were never allowed to compete, only to interrupt.

And the correction is not "switch them on". Measured across 155 signal-driven recommendations, this layer scored 6 to 10 points below random. Turning all eleven on without earning them first makes the engine worse and moves 90% further away. The fix is one currency — expected incremental margin × calibrated probability — and a coefficient each signal had to earn from the store's own history.

Section 02

One currency, one pool, one ranking

The ensemble is not a model-blending exercise. It is making every family quote its value in the same units, so that they can be compared at all — and then deleting the reserved slots, because they were only ever a workaround for families that could not be priced.

What would make this projection wrong

The signal rows are the fragile ones. If most β intervals span zero — which the price-elasticity module warns is the expected outcome at low-volume stores — the weather, calendar, news and trending row collapses toward zero and the low case falls to $850. That is a real possibility and the correct response is to publish it, drop those families, and make up the gap from sizing and overstock, which do not depend on any signal being real.

Five of ten audited stores send no cost data at all. For those stores there is no margin, so no money target can be measured — only the accuracy gate applies, and their money columns must read "no cost data", never "$0". Fixing the POS connector that drops the cost field is worth more than any model change on this page.

REC_HOLDOUT_PCT = 20 (N0.3) — one flag. It unblocks the calibration fit, the monitor, the matched gate and any causal claim at all. Zero engineering cost and it has been off for the whole programme.

Nightly on-hand snapshots (N6.3) — cannot be backfilled. Every night without it is a night that can never be used for training or grading, and it is the single confusion that caps selection accuracy near 73%.

signal_history (N4.3) — β cannot be fitted without a record of which signals were active when. Holidays, fixtures and moving dates are computable and backfill exactly; weather and news do not, so their coefficients stay refused until history exists.

The one thing not to do

Do not reach 90% by raising the p_hit floor. The engine's own settings already price that path: 0.50 gives 75.9% at 5.1 cards a run; 0.55 buys about one point, cuts coverage to 2.4 cards and halves the margin. A floor high enough to show 90% issues almost nothing, and a dashboard reading 90% beside an empty slate is a worse product than one reading 76% with five useful cards.

Abstention in N7.1 is a different thing entirely: it withholds cards whose expected profit is negative, and it publishes the coverage it chose.

How this was established. Read in source on branch Enh_Engine_v1: the four hardcoded confidences and units_at_stake=None in app/recommender/families.py; the reserved-slot arithmetic limit = max_recommendations − len(extra) in app/recommender/pipeline.py; the two trigger sets HEAT_KEYS and EVENT_KEY_PREFIXES; the twenty modules under app/signals/ with their master switches defaulting on; and the refusal discipline in app/pipeline/elasticity/guard.py that the signal coefficients should reuse. The "6 to 10 points below random over 155 signal-driven recommendations" figure is from the estate audit and is the reason this is a measurement programme rather than a feature switch.

What is projection rather than measurement. Only the $370 baseline is measured. Every other row in section 03 is an estimate with a stated range, and each has a prompt whose gate decides it. The phase durations are judgement. The claim that correcting the carrying-cost model changes the sign at two or three stores is a prediction that has to be re-run to know. Nobody has yet acted on any recommendation the engine has made, so all of it remains forecasting evidence rather than causal evidence until the holdout has been on for a quarter.
