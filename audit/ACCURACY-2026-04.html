What this page does not say

It states no holdout effect. Separating “the engine picked products that were going to sell” from “recommending them changed what sold” needs the withheld arm, and that lives on the scoreboards, which refuse the comparison until both arms carry five measured product-windows. Nothing here is evidence that showing a card caused a sale.
